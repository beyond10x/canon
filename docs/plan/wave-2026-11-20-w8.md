## Where the plan goes against the verb

The verb puts story:simulate-revise-step in its third wave, after story:canon-mutate,
story:revision-ordering and story:protocol-floor, because it shares
`crates/canon-cli/src/lib.rs`, `ess/domains/cli.yaml` and `crates/canon/src/check/space.rs` with
them. The plan runs it first and alone (plan page § Replan 9 and § Replan 12): the `sdc` session
fixed the order of the three stories that edit `crates/canon/src/simulate/mod.rs` after
story:canon-simulate, and this is the third. The stories of wave 9 are cut from the wave branch
after this unit is merged, so each reads the files as this unit leaves them.

The story was written before four things it touches landed: the child-outcome step of the walk
and its counts of open children, the subject of a child's outcome, record groups, and the
`revises` field. The brief names each and what it changes for the unit. The story's argument
that no stuck state goes unreported (§ Outcome 4) rests on record, supplied and authority steps
only adding; the unit checks it against the child-outcome and expiry steps of the walk as it is
and reports where it does not hold.

One measurement the story owes its requester (§ Decided): the count of findings of class a2 on
the other project's protocols, by the binary. The eight protocols the coordinator holds from
that project declare no `revises`, so the walk's output for them does not change; the
measurement needs that project's declarations, which the `sdc` session supplies.

The `sdc` session answered on 2026-11-21: none of its protocols declares `revises`; the unit
runs the nine protocols of that project's release 0.1.2 and reports them unchanged; the session
adds the declarations as a story of its own once a Canon release carries the revise step, and
measures class a2 then. The measurement is no longer owed by this unit.
