## What the decisions say the story implements

- D206 (decision-blocker:child-case-cycles, option 1): a static compile-time check over the
  protocols `case.open` names refuses a protocol that can reach itself (the closed-IR rule of
  `docs/design/canon-protocol-calculus-design.md` section 39.5). No run-time check: `case.open` names a
  protocol and a subject, never an existing case id.
- D207 (decision-blocker:commissions-per-case, option 1): one commission per case; a second party
  works through a child case (`case.open`). Canon knows no commission, so this fixes the story's
  premise that `case.open` is the only way a case is shared; nothing in Canon counts commissions.
